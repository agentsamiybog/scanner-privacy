Khayro Pattern Analyser — Privacy Policy

Khayro Pattern Analyser ("we") is a browser extension that provides candlestick chart analysis for the Pocket Option website. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why, and how it is handled.

1. What we collect

To identify your access account the extension stores:

  • Account identifier — a generated ID (format KHR-XXXXX) used to identify your subscription, linked to the numeric account ID visible in your Pocket Option session.
  • Access data — your subscription tier, its expiry, and your daily scan count (used to enforce plan limits).
  • Session token — stored locally in your browser to keep you signed in.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your trades, balance, or any financial information.
  • We do not track your browsing history or activity on other websites.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • Candlestick analysis is performed locally in your browser and is not transmitted to us.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. How we use your data

The data above is used solely to identify your account, manage your subscription tier, and enforce daily scan limits. It is not used for any other purpose.

4. Where your data is stored

Account data is stored securely using Supabase, a hosted database provider acting as our data processor. Access to the database is restricted to the specific server functions required to operate the service.

5. Data retention and your rights

Account data is kept while your account is active. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the address below, after which your account and associated data will be removed.

6. Contact

For any privacy request or question, contact: pocketagentdevelop@gmail.com
