Vibe Pair Sniper — Privacy Policy

Vibe Pair Sniper ("we") is a browser extension that provides pair analysis for the Pocket Option website. This policy explains exactly what the extension does with data.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Vibe Pair Sniper has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

2. What is stored on your device

The extension keeps a few settings locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local) and never sends them to us:

  • the position and size of the panel on the page;
  • the address list of the platform's public demo quote servers;
  • a random 10-character guest label generated by the extension itself, used only to open the public demo quote feed. It is not tied to your identity or to your Pocket Option account.

Removing the extension deletes this data.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not read your Pocket Option account, balance, trades or any financial information.
  • We do not place, modify or cancel trades. The extension can send only four feed commands (open the demo feed, select a pair, load quote history, keep-alive); trading commands are blocked in code.
  • We do not track your browsing history or activity on other websites. The extension is active only on pocketoption.com.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts or device identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or third-party trackers.

4. Requests to Pocket Option

To build its analysis the extension connects, from the Pocket Option page, to the platform's own public demo quote feed. It sends the pair you selected and a keep-alive signal, and receives quotes. No cookies, no credentials and none of your account data are sent.

5. Where analysis happens

All calculations are performed locally in your browser. Results are shown in the panel and are not transmitted anywhere.

6. Changes and your rights

If the extension ever starts to collect data, this policy will be updated before that version is published. Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side.

7. Contact

For any privacy request or question, contact: pocketagentdevelop@gmail.com
