Vibe Pair Sniper — Privacy Policy

Vibe Pair Sniper ("we") is a browser extension that provides pair analysis for the Pocket Option website. This policy explains exactly what the extension does with data.

1. What we store to verify your access

Analysis in Vibe Pair Sniper is available with an access plan issued by the service manager. To know which plan belongs to which copy of the extension, we keep, in our access database:

  • your access code (format VPS-XXXXX) — shown to you in the panel so that you can send it to your manager;
  • your access plan, its expiry date and the number of analyses used today (used to enforce the plan's daily limit);
  • a one-way fingerprint (SHA-256) of the installation ID described below, and the time of the first and the last check.

We do not store your name, email, phone, address or any Pocket Option account data.

2. The installation ID

On first run the extension generates a random installation ID with the browser's cryptographic random generator. It is not derived from your identity, your device or your Pocket Option account. It is stored only in your browser and is sent only to our access server over HTTPS when the extension checks your access; it is never shown on screen. Our database keeps only its fingerprint, so the ID itself cannot be recovered from it.

The ID is tied to this installation of the extension, not to your Pocket Option account. In another browser, or after you reinstall the extension or clear its data, a new ID and a new access code are created; your manager can transfer your access to the new code.

3. What is stored on your device

The extension keeps in your browser (chrome.storage.local): the position and size of the panel, your selected target pair, the address list of the platform's public demo quote servers, a random 10-character guest label used only to open the public demo quote feed, the installation ID, your access code and a session token used to check your access plan. Removing the extension deletes this data.

4. Requests to our access server

To check your access the extension sends to our access server the installation ID (when it signs in) or the session token, together with the product name, and receives your access code, plan, expiry date and the number of analyses left today. Before each analysis it asks the server to count one analysis against your plan. These requests are made by the extension's background service worker, never by the Pocket Option page. The access database is hosted by Supabase, acting as our data processor; access to it is restricted to the specific server functions required to operate the service.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not read your Pocket Option account, balance, trades or any financial information.
  • We do not place, modify or cancel trades. The extension can send only four feed commands (open the demo feed, select a pair, load quote history, keep-alive); trading commands are blocked in code.
  • We do not track your browsing history or activity on other websites. The extension is active only on pocketoption.com.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or browser fingerprints.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or third-party trackers, and we do not sell or share your data.

6. Requests to Pocket Option

To build its analysis the extension connects, from the Pocket Option page, to the platform's own public demo quote feed. It sends the pair you selected and a keep-alive signal, and receives quotes. No cookies, no credentials and none of your account data are sent.

7. Where analysis happens

All calculations are performed locally in your browser. Results are shown in the panel and are not transmitted anywhere.

8. Data retention and your rights

Access data is kept while your access code exists. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the data linked to your access code at any time by contacting us at the address below.

9. Changes

If the extension starts to collect other data, this policy will be updated before that version is published.
